## Tips to Stretch the Free 10 Proxies Further

A few practical tactics if you want to maximize what you can do under the free cap:

- **Cache aggressively.** Don't re-fetch pages you already have. Even simple SQLite caching saves enormous bandwidth.
- **Use HEAD requests for change detection.** When checking if a page changed, a HEAD request is dramatically lighter than a full GET.
- **Compress responses.** Always send `Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate, br` in your headers. Saves real bandwidth.
- **Filter at the DOM level, not the network level.** Avoid downloading images, fonts, or scripts you won't parse. Block them in Playwright/Puppeteer.
- **Stagger requests.** Use the unlimited threads but with reasonable delays. Hammering a site burns bandwidth on retries.
- **Rotate intentionally, not randomly.** Stick with one IP per session rather than shuffling on every request, unless your target requires per-request rotation.

These habits mater even after you upgrade, because bandwidth costs scale linearly with carelessness.
